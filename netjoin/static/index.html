      Laptop address

      Addresses NetJoin may give this laptop – pick a block the site doesn't use. Type 35 or .35 for short.

      Join always uses this address. If another device has it, Join stops instead of picking another.

      Gateway / DNS override

      Off = inherit from the level above (default: none). Leave a field blank with the box ticked to force none.

      Without DNS servers you can reach devices by IP, but web pages won't load.

      Discover networks on the wire

      Plug into the BMS network and run this before you know its IP range. NetJoin listens to the traffic on the cable and asks BACnet devices to identify themselves, then suggests the subnet and a free address range. It doesn't matter what IP the laptop has at the moment, so DHCP or 169.254.x is fine.

      You've picked a Wi-Fi adapter. Discover usually needs the adapter with the BMS cable plugged in.
